# Evaluation

_Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` on held-out synthetic variants. A small diagnostic set, not evidence of football expertise._

## Set-up

- **12 held-out variants** (`backend/fixtures/evaluation/ev01`–`ev12`), generated with seeds and defensive settings never used during development. Expected behaviour was declared in `scripts/generate_fixtures.py` before the first run and is stored in `_expected.json`, which no runtime code or agent reads.
  - 9 × press recorded: 4 normal press, 3 early press, 2 with a recorded cover.
  - 2 × no press recorded: the right-back's press would come after the ball has moved on.
  - 1 × event-only: all tracking removed.
- **Pipelines.** **A**: deterministic event recap (event facts only). **C-offline**: the full tool pipeline with the rule-based router, run on the pressing question plus 3 other questions per variant. **B** (single model agent) and **C-live** (multi-agent with a live model): **not run**, because no model deployment was available (Azure error 715-123420 blocked every deployment on the student subscription). No numbers are reported for them. Separately, a single live smoke run with Foundry Local (`qwen3-8b` on-device) completed the pressing question: 4 model calls, 3 tool calls, 4 claims approved, 0 rejected, about 83 s on an RTX 5070 laptop GPU. That is one run, not an evaluation.

## Results (C-offline)

- **Predeclared behaviour met:** 12 of 12 variants.
- **Completion:** 48 of 48 investigations.
- **Claims shown that fail independent re-validation:** 0 of 156.
- **Injected fabricated claims rejected by the validator:** 60 of 60. Each variant's answer was attacked with an unknown fact id, an altered number, a citation attached to an unrelated player, a number inside an interpretation, a simulated fact presented as observed, and an unlabelled simulation claim (the last two only where simulated facts exist).
- **Latency** (offline, Windows laptop): pressing question with a fresh 200-rollout simulation, median 2933 ms (max 3064 ms); questions without simulation, a few milliseconds (median across all 48: 2 ms).

## Pipeline A (deterministic event recap)

A produced 84 claims across the 12 variants, 0 of which fail validation. It never addresses the counterfactual: it can say what was passed, but not whether pressing earlier would have helped. That is the gap the metrics, simulator and challenger fill. A comparison of answer quality between A and C needs human or model judgement and was not done.

## What this does and does not show

- It shows that, on this diagnostic set, the pipeline answers when the data supports an answer and abstains when there is no pressing moment or no tracking. Every number shown traces to a code-generated fact, and the validator catches the fabrication patterns it was tested with.
- It does **not** show that the simulator's recommendations are right about football. The simulator is rule-based and uncalibrated (see MODEL_CARD). The challenger column shows how often the finding depends on the cover-delay and attacker-policy assumptions.
- **Not measured:** live-model supported-claim rate, tokens and cost (no model available); human ratings of explanations; accessibility with assistive technology.
